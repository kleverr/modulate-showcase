Velma Config

        
Use the endpoint's built-in default, or build a custom configuration (conversation types, roles, behaviors, model signals). The right pane shows exactly what's sent as config to /api/velma-2-batch (batch) or as the first frame on /api/velma-2-streaming (streaming).
